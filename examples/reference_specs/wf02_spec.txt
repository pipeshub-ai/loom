Build an AI content generation pipeline.

It should run weekly and:
1. Research what is currently being discussed in a configurable niche, keeping
   the sources behind whatever it finds
2. Write a piece tailored to each configured channel — Slack, Teams, and a
   Confluence page want different lengths and different shapes
3. Have a person review the whole set before anything is published, and publish
   what they edited rather than what they were shown
4. Publish to every configured channel, a bounded number at a time, reporting
   which landed and which failed
5. Return the topic, whether it was approved, and the per-channel outcome

Nothing may assert anything the sources do not support. A channel that fails
must not stop the others.
